MWA Lab Phase 2 — Step 2.11B2 live lifecycle/concurrency contract
Date: 2026-09-27

Scope:
- revoked current-session authorization must reject privileged signing
- revoked auth token must reject cross-session reauthorization
- valid cross-session reauthorization must remain functional
- MWA clientlib v2.0.7 permits only one outstanding JSON-RPC request per client association
- a second same-association sign_messages call while the first is outstanding must be rejected locally with UnsupportedOperationException
- the first outstanding request must still reach one approval and its returned signature must verify only against its own payload and authorized Lab address
- wallet-side simultaneous approval callbacks are covered deterministically by ApprovalCoordinatorTest; the live demo must not claim two wallet callbacks when clientlib prevents them
- closing the dApp association while approval is pending must cancel the pending approval without signing
- a fresh association after teardown must obtain a fresh approval and sign successfully
- identity reset authority-change behavior remains covered by AndroidKeystoreIdentityRepositoryInstrumentedTest
- no auth token, association token, raw payload, or raw signature may enter evidence/logcat

Out of scope:
- persistent session/timeline storage (Phase 3)
- mainnet execution
- production wallet secrets

Repository status:
NOT COMMITTED / NOT TAGGED

Revocation defense-in-depth:
- MWA Lab tracks active authorization as a session generation, independently of authorization-scope bytes.
- authorize/reauthorize success activates the current generation.
- deauthorize, association replacement, serving completion, and host close invalidate local active authority.
- privileged handlers re-check active authority before approval and after approval before signing/submission.
- sign-and-send cancellation predicate includes active-authorization state, so revocation can stop later submissions.
